libp2p over audio

Peer discovery, encrypted libp2p connections and signed broadcasts as sound. Open these on two devices in the same room, allow the microphone, turn the volume up. Source and numbers: github.com/sgtpooki/libp2p-audio-protocol.

🔊 Audio QRA host plays a link through a speaker; every phone listening gets it. Guests: just tap Start listening. Hosts: add ?mode=host. 💬 Phone ↔ laptop chatAnnounce your PeerRecord, discover the other device, open an encrypted libp2p connection over sound, chat and ping.
