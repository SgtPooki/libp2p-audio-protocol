🔊 libp2p over audio

Peer discovery, encrypted libp2p connections, and signed broadcasts, carried as sound between the devices in a room. No network, no pairing, no app store: open a page, allow the microphone, turn the volume up. Source, measurements and the threat model: github.com/sgtpooki/libp2p-audio-protocol.

Every demo needs HTTPS (this site has it, so iPhone Safari works), a microphone permission, and two devices within earshot. Tap the matching role on each device.

1. A link for everyone in the room (Audio QR)

A presenter, a TV, a game lobby: play a URL through a speaker and every phone listening gets a tappable card. Like a QR code for people who can't see the screen. Unsigned links take ~5 s; signed ones name the sender's key.

🎧 I'm listening (guest) 📢 I'm the host host with a sample link
  • On the host, tick fast + voice for phones and speakers, add ultrasonic to be near-silent, or pick a jingle so all anyone hears is Westminster Quarters / Shave and a Haircut / Charge! / Beethoven's 5th with the link hidden under it.
  • Guests listen on every preset at once; nothing to configure.

2. Who's really here? (check-in by sound)

The host asks "who's here?" as a signed sound; phones in earshot answer by themselves with a signed reply that carries the question's nonce. The host gets a list of keys that were in the room at that moment: a screenshot or a forwarded link can't fake it. Attendance, raffles, roll call.

🙋 I'm the host 📱 I'm a guest host, hidden under "Shave and a Haircut"

3. Two devices talk libp2p over sound (chat)

Discover the other device from its signed PeerRecord, open an encrypted libp2p connection (one round trip, ~5-35 s depending on the sound preset), then chat and ping over a real libp2p stream. The same page on both devices.

💬 default: voice, auto-upgrades to fast dual (33 B/s) tri (48 B/s) turbo (67 B/s, quiet rooms) ultrasonic (near-silent) music (an arpeggio) robust (echoey rooms, radios) hidden under Big Ben
  • Both devices must use the same link. Announce me on one, then Connect on the other; Who's here? pings over the open connection.
  • Sign-only mode for amateur radio: add &encrypt=0 on both. Stock Noise + yamux instead of the lean handshake: &handshake=noise.
  • Loop repeats a message as fountain-coded chunks so a late listener still gets it; Hide in music plays any audio file under the transmission.
